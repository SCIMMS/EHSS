# Rights and third-party materials

This private research deposit does not add an open-source or open-data license.
No blanket permission to redistribute third-party material is granted here.
Contact the corresponding author for reuse terms until the authors approve a
release license and public data/software statement.

Molecular structures originate from the Protein Data Bank. The retained entry
identifiers, source URLs, primary citations, chain selections, exclusions and
hashes are recorded in `data/benchmark/input_provenance.json`. Cite the relevant
PDB entries and original structural studies when using these coordinates.

IMoS 1.13 numerical outputs and configuration records are retained as benchmark
evidence. The IMoS executable and MATLAB Runtime are not redistributed; a vendor
rerun requires a separately obtained compatible installation. Python dependency
packages are not vendored and retain their own licenses.

The manuscript remains an author-review draft. Funding and conflict-of-interest
text follow the supplied JSR submission; the repository does not independently
verify funding attribution or coauthor approval.
